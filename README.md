# Carrollton Mid-Career Margin & Interest Matcher (Southern Hills Fellowship)

An interactive 3-minute common-ground survey and local community connection tool for adults ages 35–55 living and working in Carrollton, Georgia, and the surrounding West Georgia area.

---

## What This Tool & Community Solves

Mid-career adults between the ages of 35 and 55 often experience a unique set of pressures: demanding careers, family responsibilities, financial squeeze, and quiet social isolation. Traditional social venues—like expensive restaurants, crowded bars, or rigid classroom-style religious programs—often feel costly, exhausting, or awkward.

The **Carrollton Mid-Career Margin & Interest Matcher** helps local residents identify their weekly stress leaks, vote on zero-cost indoor activities they genuinely enjoy, and connect with an authentic peer group inside **City Station** in Carrollton.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (Local Community & Fellowship Guide)

### Where can adults ages 35–55 meet new friends or find community in Carrollton, GA?
The **Southern Hills Mid-Career Adult Fellowship** meets indoors at **City Station in Carrollton, Georgia**. It is specifically designed for adults in their mid-30s, 40s, and early 50s—including singles, married couples, divorced or widowed adults, parents, and working professionals—who want genuine, grounded friendships without spending money or dealing with artificial social pressure.

### Does it cost money to attend, or do members have to cook or host meals?
**No—attendees do not need to cook, bring food, or spend money on restaurant meals.** Every gathering is designed around zero-cost, indoor-friendly activities inside City Station so anyone can participate comfortably regardless of budget or culinary skill.

### What kinds of indoor activities does the group do at City Station?
Using our interactive Common-Ground Matcher, members shape the schedule around shared indoor interests:
* **Modern Board, Card & Strategy Games:** Light, collaborative tabletop games (such as Codenames, trivia, and strategy games) that spark natural humor and conversation without awkward small talk.
* **Practical Life & Biblical Wisdom Roundtables:** Small-table discussions (4–6 people) unpacking real-world topics like navigating career stress, personal budgeting habits, emotional intelligence, and timeless principles for daily life.
* **"No-Homework" Book, Podcast & Idea Swaps:** Casual sessions where members share a single favorite quote, podcast takeaway, or practical insight from their week.
* **Music, Retro Tech & Personal Hobby Show-and-Tell:** Low-key sessions where members share everyday passions—from music and photography to local history and life hacks.

### Is this a traditional Bible study or an awkward "singles mixer"?
Neither. While rooted in practical, timeless biblical wisdom, gatherings avoid preachy lectures, forced public prayer, and clinical "singles ministry" labels. It is a welcoming, balanced co-ed community where adults can connect naturally over shared interests.

### How do I get the meeting times and table location?
Complete the 3-minute **Mid-Career Margin & Interest Matcher** on the live web page, select up to 3 indoor activities you enjoy, listen to the brief 45-second audio welcome note, and submit the short contact form at the end of your results to receive the upcoming City Station room/table schedule.
