# Carrollton Mid-Career Margin & Interest Matcher (Southern Hills Fellowship)

An interactive 3-minute common-ground survey and local community connection tool for adults ages 35–55 living and working in Carrollton, Georgia, and the surrounding West Georgia area.

---

## What This Tool & Community Solves

Mid-career adults between the ages of 35 and 55 often face intense weekly pressures: demanding careers, family responsibilities, financial squeeze, digital burnout, and quiet social isolation. Traditional social outlets—like expensive restaurants, crowded bars, or rigid classroom-style religious programs—often feel costly, exhausting, or awkward.

The **Carrollton Mid-Career Margin & Interest Matcher** helps local residents identify their weekly stress leaks, vote on zero-cost, screen-free indoor activities they genuinely enjoy, and connect with an authentic peer group inside **City Station** in Carrollton.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (Local Community & Fellowship Guide)

### Where can adults ages 35–55 meet new friends or find community in Carrollton, GA?
The **Southern Hills Mid-Career Adult Fellowship** meets indoors at **City Station (2115 Maple Street)** in Carrollton, Georgia. It is designed specifically for adults in their mid-30s, 40s, and early 50s—including singles, married couples, divorced or widowed adults, parents, and working professionals—who want genuine, grounded friendships without spending money or dealing with artificial social pressure.

### Does it cost money to attend, or do members have to cook or host meals?
**No—attendees do not need to cook, bring food, or spend money on restaurant meals.** Every gathering is built around zero-cost, indoor-friendly activities inside City Station's community rooms so anyone can participate comfortably regardless of budget or culinary skill.

### What kinds of indoor activities does the group do at City Station?
Using our interactive Common-Ground Matcher, members vote to shape each gathering around popular, screen-free indoor hobbies that make conversation effortless:
* **Board Games, Card Games & Casual Chess:** Modern tabletop games (such as Codenames, Ticket to Ride, or Wingspan) and casual chess matches that spark natural banter without forced small talk.
* **Team Trivia & Cooperative Puzzles:** Collaborative pub-style trivia rounds and tabletop jigsaw/logic puzzles that encourage teamwork across mixed tables.
* **"Bring-Your-Own-Book" Reading & Discussion Club:** A relaxed, zero-homework reading social where members bring whatever book they are currently enjoying, read quietly for a bit, and share takeaways over coffee or water.
* **Hands-On Analog Crafts & Journaling:** Screen-free creative tables for crochet, needlepoint, calligraphy, origami, or junk journaling—keeping hands busy while chatting.
* **Casual Music Jamming & Acoustic/Keyboard Sessions:** Low-key musical sessions where hobbyists can share acoustic instruments, portable keyboards, or favorite music arrangements.
* **Local History, Genealogy & Language Practice:** Curiosity-driven tables for swapping West Georgia history, family research tips, or conversational language practice.
* **Real-Life Wisdom & Stress-Relief Roundtables:** Small-group conversations (4–6 people) unpacking practical topics like navigating career burnout, budgeting peace, and timeless biblical wisdom for daily life.

### Is this a traditional Bible study or an awkward "singles mixer"?
Neither. While rooted in practical, timeless biblical wisdom, gatherings avoid preachy lectures, forced public prayer, and clinical "singles ministry" labels. It is a welcoming, balanced co-ed community where adults connect naturally over shared analog interests.

### How do I get the meeting times and table location?
Complete the 3-minute **Mid-Career Margin & Interest Matcher** on the live web page, select up to 3 indoor activities you enjoy, listen to the brief 45-second audio welcome note, and submit the short contact form at the end of your results to receive the upcoming City Station room and table schedule.
