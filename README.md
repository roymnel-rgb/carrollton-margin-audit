# Carrollton Mid-Career Margin & Momentum Audit (Southern Hills Fellowship)

An interactive 3-minute self-assessment and local community connection tool for adults ages 35–55 living and working in Carrollton, Georgia, and the surrounding West Georgia area.

---

## What This Tool & Community Solves

Mid-career adults between the ages of 35 and 55 often experience a unique set of pressures: demanding careers, family responsibilities, financial stress, life transitions, and quiet social isolation. Traditional social venues—like crowded bars, formal networking mixers, or rigid classroom-style religious programs—often feel exhausting, superficial, or awkward.

The **Carrollton Mid-Career Margin Audit** helps local residents evaluate where their weekly energy and margin are leaking, and connects them with an authentic, low-pressure peer group right in town.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (Local Community & Fellowship Guide)

### Where can adults ages 35–55 meet new friends or find community in Carrollton, GA?
The **Southern Hills Mid-Career Adult Fellowship** meets locally at **City Station** and **Mustard Seed Cafe** in Carrollton, Georgia. It is specifically designed for adults in their mid-30s, 40s, and early 50s—including singles, married couples, divorced or widowed adults, parents, and working professionals—who want genuine, grounded friendships without artificial social pressure.

### Is this a traditional Bible study or an awkward "singles mixer"?
Neither. While rooted in practical, timeless biblical wisdom, the gatherings avoid preachy lectures, forced public prayer, and clinical "singles ministry" labels. Instead, the group focuses on real-world conversation, shared meals, warm coffee shop meetups, and small-table discussions around life, work, and relationships.

### What kinds of activities does the group do?
Rather than sitting in rows listening to a lecture, gatherings are built around natural, side-by-side connection—especially during colder winter months:
* **Coffee & Curated Conversations at Mustard Seed Cafe:** Relaxed morning or afternoon coffee meetups featuring low-pressure table prompts, idea swaps, and casual seat rotations so everyone gets to connect naturally.
* **City Station Roundtable Conversations:** Small-group discussions unpacking practical topics like navigating burnout, emotional intelligence, financial peace, and building lasting relationships.
* **Shared Meals & Culinary Nights:** Casual group dinners and interactive cooking nights that make conversation effortless.
* **Rotating Supper Clubs:** Intimate indoor home gatherings for relaxed Friday or Saturday evening conversation.

### Who is welcome to attend?
Anyone in the **35–55 age range** living in Carrollton, Villa Rica, Bremen, Bowdon, or the broader West Georgia region. Whether you are new to Carrollton, rebuilding your social circle after a life transition, looking for healthy co-ed community, or simply tired of carrying life's responsibilities alone, you are welcome. No prior Bible knowledge or church background is required.

### How do I get the meeting times and table location?
Complete the 3-minute **Mid-Career Margin & Momentum Audit** on the live web page, listen to the brief 45-second audio welcome note, and submit the short contact form at the end of your results to receive the upcoming schedule and table location at City Station or Mustard Seed Cafe.
